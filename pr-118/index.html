We leverage data science and molecular simulation to explore molecular systems with changing and complex phase behavior — addressing open problems in computational chemical and materials sciences, all to better our world.

Our group is committed to open science and FAIR data standards. This site is built by our entire lab — from undergrads to PI — as a living practice in version control and software collaboration.
